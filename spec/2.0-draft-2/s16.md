# 16. Health and Education Context Profiles

## 16.1 Scope

This section defines two profiles:

| Profile | Identifier | Category |
|----------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------|
| Health Context Profile | https://hcp.example/spec/profiles/health-context/2.0 | `health_context` |
| Education Context Profile | https://hcp.example/spec/profiles/education-context/2.0 | `education_learner_profile` |

- An HCP Provider that accepts `issuer-attested` entries in `health_context` MUST conform to the Health Context Profile. One that accepts `issuer-attested` entries in `education_learner_profile` MUST conform to the Education Context Profile (18.1).
- Each profile requires the VC Evidence Profile (14). An implementation claiming either lists the VC Evidence Profile as well.
- An HCP Provider that accepts only `person-declared` and `model-inferred` entries in a category need not claim its profile, and MAY claim it.

## 16.2 Health Context Profile

An implementation conforming to this profile MUST treat all entries in `health_context` as high-sensitivity regardless of content.

Because `health_context` is high-sensitivity, a copy disclosed from it is usable for at most 24 hours (7.6). That is sensitivity's first normative effect, and it is in Core.

Access tokens issued under a grant whose scopes include `health_context` SHOULD NOT have a lifetime exceeding 1 hour, the default in 13.4.

An `issuer-attested` entry in `health_context` MUST carry a credential, as its content or in support of it (4.2.2), and at least one credential it carries MUST pass verification at ingest (14.6). A write that does not is rejected: with invalid request where it carries no credential, and with credential invalid where none passes. Entries the Context Subject declared, and `model-inferred` entries, are unaffected.

The implementation's `credential_status_interval` MUST NOT exceed 1 hour (14.7, 19).

A `model-inferred` entry in `health_context` MUST NOT be disclosed to a Context Client unless the most recent confirmation record on its current version is `confirmed` (3.8). Until the Context Subject confirms it, a query does not return it and a get entry by `ref` fails with not authorized, as for an entry that does not exist (4.2.4). A rejected one is never disclosed. The implementation SHOULD offer the Context Subject a way to review unconfirmed health inferences through a Management Client. An unconfirmed inference about a person's health is new health information about them, which they may never have disclosed, and a wrong one can shape care.

Deployment practice for health pilots, including the reasons for shorter token lifetimes and the custody of documents received before a grant, is in the health guide (23).

## 16.3 How v1's Commitment Is Met

v1 committed that VC-Extended conformance --- now the VC Evidence Profile (14) --- would be required for health and education pilots at v2. It is met as follows:

- The claims the commitment is about are institutional ones: a clinician's assessment, an assessing platform's record of mastery. Under these profiles each such claim carries a credential its issuer signed, which the provider verifies and whose status it keeps (14.6, 14.7).
- A provider that holds institutional health or education claims claims the profile for that category, and with it the VC Evidence Profile (16.1).
- A person's own statements and a model's inferences are not required to carry a credential. Requiring one would put a signature requirement on what a person says about themselves.

## 16.4 Education Context Profile

This profile adds rules for implementations holding institutional education context. It is identified as https://hcp.example/spec/profiles/education-context/2.0 (2.7).

An implementation conforming to this profile:

- MUST conform to the VC Evidence Profile (14).
- MUST verify credentials secured with `ecdsa-sd-2023` as well as `eddsa-rdfc-2022` (14.5), the two cryptosuites Open Badges 3.0 verifiers support.
- MUST support the Open Badges 3.0 context for education entries.
- MUST check credential status under 14.7, including the Bitstring Status List entries Open Badges 3.0 credentials carry.

An `issuer-attested` entry in `education_learner_profile` MUST carry a credential, as its content or in support of it (4.2.2), and at least one credential it carries MUST pass verification at ingest (14.6). A write that does not is rejected: with invalid request where it carries no credential, and with credential invalid where none passes. Entries the learner declared, and `model-inferred` entries, are unaffected.

For credentials intended to be verified outside the deployment that issues them, the embedded Data Integrity mechanism has the broadest support among current wallets and verifiers, and 1EdTech notes that less common proof mechanisms may not be accepted everywhere (22).
