# 22. Education Guide

This guide assembles Core, the General Context Profile (11), the Education Context Profile (16.4), and the VC Evidence Profile (14) for education pilots.

## 22.1 Where Learner Context Goes

v1's education example carried a learner's mastery, goal, pacing, learning preferences, and accessibility needs inside one credential. In v2 they are separated by who asserts them and what evidence supports them.

| Content | Entry | Category | Declaration type | Context Asserter |
|------------------------------------|-------------------------------|------------------------------|--------------------|------------------------------|
| Mastery of a skill, with a score | An Open Badges 3.0 credential as the entry's content (14.4) | `education_learner_profile` | `issuer-attested` | The issuing platform |
| Current placement level | A statement, or a credential as the content if the platform issues one | `education_learner_profile` | `issuer-attested` | The assessing platform (3.7) |
| Goal, such as a placement exam in eight weeks | A statement (11) | `goals_and_constraints` | `person-declared` | The learner |
| Pacing and learning preferences | A statement (11) | `education_learner_profile` | `person-declared` | The learner |
| Accessibility needs relevant to learning | A statement (11) | `education_learner_profile` | `person-declared` | The learner |

Category determines which grants reach an entry (4.5). Placing the goal in `goals_and_constraints` means a tutoring client needs that scope as well, and the learner sees it requested as a separate category. A pilot that wants the goal read under the learner-profile grant alone can place it in `education_learner_profile`.

Accommodations and accessibility needs may carry a different sensitivity from mastery data. Whether they warrant a category or facet of their own is part of the vocabulary review (26); pilots should record what they chose.

A learning preference ("Worked examples first; graphs help; progressive hints") and an accessibility need ("Large text and high contrast") are written the same way, each as its own entry with its own dimension, so the learner can correct or delete one without touching the others.

## 22.3 Credentials

- Mastery and achievement credentials are Open Badges 3.0 credentials under the VC Evidence Profile (14.4). v1 committed that VC Evidence would be required for education pilots at v2. The Education Context Profile meets it: a platform's record of mastery carries a credential, while what a learner says about themselves does not need one (16.3, 16.4).
- Where credentials are intended to be verified outside the pilot, prefer the embedded Data Integrity mechanism (14.5, 16.4). It has the broadest support among current wallets and verifiers, and 1EdTech notes that less common proof mechanisms may not be accepted everywhere.
- For payloads inside education entries, established objects exist and should be considered before anything bespoke: xAPI statements and IEEE LearnerState objects for learning activity and state, the Comprehensive Learner Record and Open Badges 3.0 for achievement, and 1EdTech's Competencies and Academic Skills Exchange (CASE) for the competency frameworks they refer to. Pilots should record which payload shapes they used (26).
- A large Comprehensive Learner Record and a set of granular Open Badges can carry the same information with different consent properties. The bundle is a single share-or-not decision; the unbundled set can be released piece by piece. A narrowed grant (6.1.5.1) is one way to release part of a bundle without splitting it. A credential secured with `ecdsa-sd-2023` is another: the provider can disclose only the claims a request needs (14.9). Pilots should record which form they encountered and whether learners wanted finer control than it allowed.

## 22.4 Guardian Consent

In Core, the Authorizing Principal is the Context Subject (3.3). A grant a parent or guardian authorizes for a learner is conformant under the Delegated Authorization Profile (17):

- The guardian is a delegate. The Authorization Service establishes the delegation and records its basis, such as an enrollment record or the school's own attestation of the relationship (17.3). A school is never the delegate: it acts through its HCP Clients under the grants the guardian authorizes (17.1).
- Scope the delegation to what the deployment needs, typically `education_learner_profile` and `goals_and_constraints`. A learner's `health_context` is in scope only where the basis covers it (17.4).
- Set `term.atMajority` for a guardian of a minor. At the age of majority every grant the guardian authorized lapses, and the learner sees a summary of the delegated period and re-authorizes what they want (17.6). The guardian keeps read access to the record of the grants they authorized (17.7).
- Whether the learner may also authorize grants, and from what age, is a deployment decision under the law that applies (17.5). The learner can always revoke a grant made for them.
- Pilots holding v1 guardian grants carry them over with an unspecified basis, and establish new delegations under 17.2 before issuing new grants (17.8).
